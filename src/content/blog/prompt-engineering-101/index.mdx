# Prompt Engineering 101: The Techniques That Actually Work

Prompt engineering sounds intimidating. It's not. It's simply learning what kinds of instructions help AI models think more clearly and produce more useful outputs.

Here are the four techniques with the highest real-world leverage.

---

## Technique 1: Role Assignment

Assigning a specific role primes the model to draw on relevant domain knowledge and communication style.

**Basic:** "Explain blockchain to me."

**With Role:** "Act as a CTO explaining blockchain to a non-technical board of directors. Use no jargon. Focus on business implications, not technical mechanisms. Keep it under 200 words."

Role assignment works because LLMs have absorbed vast amounts of domain-specific text during training. Telling it what lens to use activates that knowledge.

---

## Technique 2: Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting

For complex reasoning tasks, explicitly ask the model to show its reasoning process before giving the answer.

```text
Before answering, reason through this step by step:
[Problem]
First, identify all relevant factors.
Then, weigh each factor.
Finally, give me your conclusion and confidence level.
```

Studies show CoT prompting improves accuracy by 40-70% on reasoning and math tasks.

---

## Technique 3: Few-Shot Examples

Showcase 2-3 examples of what good output looks like before asking for the real thing.

```text
Here are two examples of the format I want:

Example 1:
Input: [show input]
Output: [show ideal output]

Example 2:
Input: [show input]
Output: [show ideal output]

Now do the same for:
Input: [your actual input]
```

Few-shot examples are especially powerful for maintaining consistent style across multiple outputs.

---

## Technique 4: Output Constraints

Specifying format, length, and structure dramatically improves usability.

```text
Respond ONLY with:
1. A bullet-point summary (max 5 points)
2. A confidence score (Low / Medium / High)
3. One follow-up question I should ask

Do NOT include preamble or explanation.
```

---

## The Master Template (All Four Combined)

```text
Act as a [ROLE] with [X years] of experience in [DOMAIN].

Context: [Provide rich background on the situation]

Task: [Clearly state what you need]

Constraints:
- [Length/format requirement]
- [Tone/style requirement]
- [What to avoid]

Reason through this step by step before giving your final answer.

Format your response as:
- [Section 1]
- [Section 2]
- [Section 3]
```

This template consistently produces exceptional results across virtually any domain. Bookmark it.
