## 🌟 What is LLM For Everyone?

**LLM For Everyone** ([llmforeveryone.netlify.app](https://llmforeveryone.netlify.app/)) is an open-source, client-side interactive platform built to eliminate the gap between frontier Artificial Intelligence models and real-world daily execution.

While Large Language Models (LLMs) such as OpenAI's **ChatGPT**, Anthropic's **Claude**, Google's **Gemini**, and xAI's **Grok** are transforming industries, most people encounter two major friction points:
1. **Generic "AI Slop"**: Broad, one-sentence prompts produce generic, unhelpful outputs that require hours of rewriting.
2. **Domain Disconnect**: Most AI resources are tailored strictly for machine learning engineers, leaving educators, doctors, nurses, farmers, homemakers, and students without domain-tested, actionable workflows.

**LLM For Everyone solves this.** Within 60 seconds, users are guided through an adaptive discovery pathway that matches their exact discipline, educational tier, and workflow bottlenecks with production-grade, context-engineered prompt recipes and curated AI toolstacks.

## 🎯 Who is it for?

### 🎓 1. Students & Academic Researchers (K-12 to PhD)
- **High School / K-12**: Socratic conceptual tutors using the Feynman technique, adaptive exam timetables, and active-recall flashcard builders.
- **College Undergraduate (UG)**: Algorithmic code dry-runs, asymptotic analysis ($O(n)$ complexity), lab report structuring, and capstone architecture blueprints.
- **Postgraduate (PG / Master’s)**: Harvard-style business case solvers, unit economics modeling, and thesis proposal structuring.
- **PhD & Doctoral Researchers**: Systematic literature review matrices, research gap identification, methodology critique, and formal journal peer-review rebuttal drafting.

### 💼 2. Working Professionals & Life Roles
- **Doctors & Clinicians**: Clinical SOAP note drafting, differential diagnosis exploration, and plain-English patient discharge instructions.
- **Nurses & Healthcare Staff**: SBAR shift handoffs, triage escalation summaries, and NANDA-standardized nursing care plans.
- **Teachers & Educators**: 5E differentiated lesson plans, multi-tiered classroom activities, and custom grading rubrics.
- **Software Developers**: Microservice architecture reviews, automated test suite generation, legacy refactoring, and SQL query optimization.
- **Marketing Heads & Growth Leads**: High-converting direct response hooks, full-funnel campaign briefs, and SEO semantic cluster maps.
- **Founders & Startups**: 10-slide venture pitch deck narratives, bottom-up TAM calculators, and customer discovery scripts.
- **Farmers & Agriculturalists**: Multi-season crop rotation planners, soil amendment schedules, bio-fertilizer protocols, and agricultural grant proposals.
- **Homemakers & Family Managers**: Zero-waste pantry-to-plate weekly meal planners, 50/30/20 household budget optimizers, and kids' home STEM learning activities.
- **Lawyers & Legal Counsel**: Contract redlining risk matrices, cross-examination deposition questions, and case law synthesis memos.
- **Financial Analysts**: Three-statement financial modeling, variance commentary narratives, and DCF sensitivity matrices.
- **Designers & Creatives**: UX copy error matrices, design system token specifications, and Midjourney style prompt formulas.
- **Career Pivoters & Job Seekers**: Transferable skill translation, Google XYZ-formula resume bullet rewrites, and mock interview prep.

## ⚡ Core Capabilities & Value

- 🧭 **60-Second Guided Discovery Wizard**: Independent, dedicated pathways for students and professionals with zero category mixing.
- ✍️ **In-Browser Interactive Prompt Customizer**:
  - **Variable Substitution (`Fill Variables`)**: Real-time bracket substitution for custom parameters (`[variable]`).
  - **Direct In-Place Editing (`Edit Text`)**: Edit constraints, customize prompts directly on the website before copying or testing.
- 🚀 **1-Click Launchers Across 4 Leading LLMs**: Direct deep-links to launch customized prompts in **ChatGPT**, **Claude**, **Gemini**, and **Grok**.
- 🛠️ **AI Tools Directory with DuckDuckGo Favicons**: Profiled tools across coding, research, legal, design, marketing, and finance with auto-resolved icons.
- 📚 **Thought Leadership & Path Adoption Guide**: Practical principles for context engineering, understanding hallucination boundaries, and compounding productivity.
- 📰 **Blog & MDX Knowledge Hub**: Community-contributed guides on prompt engineering techniques and domain workflows with full table and code block support.

## 🔒 Privacy & Ethical AI Notice

- **100% In-Browser Privacy**: LLM For Everyone has **zero backend databases, zero user logins, zero trackers, and zero prompt loggers**. All prompt customizations live strictly in your local browser session.
- **⚠️ AI Hallucination & Verification Notice**: Large Language Models can hallucinate, omit critical domain context, or state inaccurate facts with confidence. Always apply your critical judgment, domain expertise, and cross-verify all AI-generated code, medical advice, legal citations, financial models, and factual claims before real-world reliance.
